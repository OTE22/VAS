Model Training

The ML similarity model learns from your feedback when you approve or reject merge suggestions.

How it works:

  • Each approval/rejection collects training data automatically
  • After collecting enough samples (default: 50), you can train the model
  • The trained model improves merge suggestion accuracy over time
